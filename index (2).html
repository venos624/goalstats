How it works

1. Shots on targetFor each team we take the average shots on target it creates and allows per match this season.
2. Expected chancesHome team’s expected shots on target = its own average for + the away team’s average against, divided by 2. The same for the away team.
3. The gapThe difference tells you who is expected to create more. 2+ is a clear favourite; under 0.5 is an even match.
