Upcoming matches

Average goals per match for each team. Expected home goals = (home team goals for + away team goals against) / 2, and the same for the away team. Times in your local time. Click a column to sort.
What each column means
For / Against
Average goals the team scored / conceded per match, over the matches you picked (all, last 10, 5 or 3).
Venue
With “Home team at home, away team away”, only the home team’s home matches and the away team’s away matches count.
Exp. H / A / diff
Expected home goals = (home goals for + away goals against) / 2. Expected away goals = (away goals for + home goals against) / 2. Diff = home minus away: positive means the home team is expected to be better, negative the away team. A gap of 1 goal or more is highlighted; under 0.25 is close to a coin flip.
Over 2.5 / BTTS
Share of both teams’ matches (same sample) with 3+ goals / with both sides scoring.
Games
How many matches were counted for each team. With few matches, averages swing a lot by chance.
Expected gap / Favourite
Show only matches where one team is expected to be better by at least that many goals, and/or where the expected favourite is the home or the away team.
Odds
Average market odds (1, X, 2 and Over 2.5) as published with the fixtures.

Averages over the last 3–5 matches are noisy; full-season averages usually say more about a team than recent form.
